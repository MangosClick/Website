## The studio
- MANGOS is a small website studio building websites for local businesses in India. Website: mangos.click.- One small team designs, writes and builds every site, from the first call to launch. Visitors deal with the people doing the work.
- Who we build for: clinics, cafés, tutors, contractors, real estate, garages, shops, salons, studios. Businesses that have outgrown word of mouth.
- Every site is fast on a phone, easy to find on Google and set up to turn visitors into calls, chats and bookings.
- Built with hand-written code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript), open-source motion libraries (GSAP, Lenis), Google Fonts, Node.js and Claude AI to speed up the build. No page builders, nothing the client is locked into.
- Currently booking new projects (two build slots open this month).

## What we build
- Business websites: custom-designed, 5 to 15 pages. Custom design, mobile first, fast, easy to edit.
- Starter sites: 1 to 5 pages for shops, tutors and single-service businesses going online for the first time. Contact form, maps, WhatsApp chat. Live in about two weeks.
- Redesigns: the current site rebuilt with a fresh look, clearer words, faster pages, same domain.
- Get found locally: on-page SEO basics, Google Business Profile, analytics.
- Landing pages: one focused page for a launch, offer or ad campaign.
- Timelines: starter and business websites 2 to 5 weeks; redesigns and landing pages 1 to 3 weeks.

## Process
1. Talk it through: a short call about the business, its customers and what the site must do.
2. Plan and design: a page plan, the words, and a design the client checks on their own phone before we build.
3. Build and launch: built mobile-first, connected to the client's domain, switched on once they sign it off.
4. Hand it over: the client gets the domain, code and logins, plus a short guide to updating it themselves.

## FAQ
- How long does a website take? A starter site is usually live in about two weeks. A larger business site takes three to five weeks. The date is given in writing before work begins.
- What do I need to give you? A logo if you have one, a few photos and a rough idea of what you sell. We help write the words.
- Will I own the website? Yes. Domain, code and content are 100% the client's. If they leave, they take all of it.
- Can I update it myself? Business sites come with a simple editor for text and images. Bigger changes are quoted per job.
- Hosting and domain? We set both up in the client's name, or work with what they already have.
- Will my site show up on Google? Every site ships with the basics done right: fast pages, clean headings, page titles and a Google Business Profile. Ranking takes time.
- We reply to every enquiry within one working day.
