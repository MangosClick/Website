Our services

What do we do?

General dentistry & prophylaxis

A smile can change worlds — and healthy teeth can last a lifetime! That's why good preventive dental care is essential, and it starts with children. Whether it's the annual check-up or regular prophylaxis — together we lay the foundation for your dental health.

Conservative dentistry

Sometimes it happens anyway: a cavity or root inflammation makes life difficult. With conservative treatments we help you repair your natural teeth and keep them healthy for as long as possible.

Dental prosthetics

The perfect prosthesis is the one that fits your needs. We'll find the best solution for you: from removable dentures to crowns, bridges and implants, we offer a range of options for professional tooth replacement.

Aesthetics

Charming someone with a bright white smile or grinning contentedly at yourself in the mirror is a pretty great feeling — and a real confidence boost! With aesthetic dentistry we help you get there.

Periodontology

Healthy periodontal tissue is the basis for teeth that sit firmly. When periodontitis attacks gums and bone, we have to step in! With professional periodontal treatment, gum corrections and conservative care, we offer ideal therapy options.

In-house lab

In our own laboratory we can adjust and repair dentures, veneers and ceramic inlays quickly and precisely. Dental technicians and dentists work closely together to achieve perfect results without long waits.
