What do we do?

General dentistry & prophylaxis

  • Thorough examinations and professional teeth cleaning
  • Individual prophylaxis, including brushing lessons and fluoride treatments — especially for expectant mothers and diabetics
  • Efficient appointment management with minimal waiting times

Conservative dentistry

  • Fillings, motorised root canal treatment with endo motors, inlays and onlays
  • State-of-the-art equipment for precise, minimally invasive procedures

Prosthetics & implants

  • Crowns, bridges and removable dentures
  • Conventional and ceramic implants
  • Digital impressions and our own lab for faster, more accurate restorations

Aesthetics

  • Teeth whitening, tooth jewellery, aligners (Invisalign etc.) and veneers
  • Modern cosmetic solutions to beautify your smile
More on bleaching

Periodontology

  • Comprehensive periodontal treatment, including gum corrections
  • Maintaining a healthy foundation for your teeth

In-house lab

  • CBCT (cone beam CT), OPG (panoramic X-ray), intraoral scanner and digital X-ray
  • Our own lab with milling machine, 3D printer and model scanner
