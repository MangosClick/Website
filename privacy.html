1. What we collect

1.1 Details you give us

When you fill in a form, book a call or email us, we keep what you send. That usually means:

  • Your name and work email
  • Company name and role
  • A phone number, if you choose to share one
  • Anything you write in a message to us

1.2 Details collected automatically

Like most websites, ours records basic technical information when you visit:

  • Approximate location based on IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • The page that sent you here
  • Which pages you viewed and for how long

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and run projects we have agreed on
  • To send updates you asked for, and nothing else
  • To understand which pages are useful so we can improve them
  • To keep the site secure and spot abuse

3. Who else sees it

We never sell personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that host this site, send our email or store our files, under contracts that limit how they use it
  • Authorities, where the law requires us to
  • A buyer or successor, if the business itself is ever sold or merged

4. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies for analytics and to remember preferences. You can block them in your browser settings; the site will still work.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't turn into projects are deleted after twelve months. Project records are kept for as long as accounting and contract law require.

6. Security

Access to client data is limited to the people working on your project and protected with two-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise absolute protection.

7. Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may be able to:

  • See the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Opt out of any marketing email
  • Withdraw consent you gave earlier, by writing to hello@mangos.click

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Big changes will also be announced by email to active clients.
